# Rewrite rules

## The formula

> Accomplished [X] against [baseline], measured by [Y], by doing [Z]. Optional: proved by [W].

This is Laszlo Bock's XYZ formula (Google, 2014), updated for the agent era.

- Outcome and baseline first: "Cut weekly reporting from 3 days to 4 hours by..."
- The person is the subject: "I built and tested X", "Led the team that...". Never "Agents built X".
- Agents at most once in the summary and once per role. Mention them only where the speed gain is the point.
- "Proved by" is optional. W must be something a stranger could check: a public link, a named test set, real users, a live metric, a named reviewer. If nothing like that exists, leave it out. Don't suggest "available on request".
- Named tools go in the skills list. Proof goes in the bullets.

## Never invent

- No new numbers, tools, product names, dates, team sizes or claims.
- If the formula needs a fact you don't have, write `[ASK: the exact fact]`, e.g. `[ASK: starting level before the 300% rise]`.
- No fake percentages. If there's no hard number, use scale, frequency, time or before and after, and only if the CV or the person gave it.
- Keep the person's real numbers exactly as written. Don't round, convert or combine them.

## Honest verbs

| What happened | Verb to use |
|---|---|
| They did it alone | built, wrote, designed, shipped, cut, grew |
| They led a team that did it | Led the team that..., Ran..., ...while I led the X team |
| They were part of a team | Co-led, Part of the team that..., My team... |
| They advised or helped | Advised on, Helped, Supported |
| They designed and ran a product someone else coded | Designed and ran, Took [product] from X to Y as its product lead |

When a team result is written with a solo verb on the CV, fix it in the rewrite and say why in the change list. An honest verb with a real number beats a big verb with a doubt behind it.

## Cuts framing

"Restructured", "reduced costs", "reduced headcount", "streamlined the team" read as cuts. Reword toward what the change made possible, but only with facts from the CV or the person:
- If they told you the outcome: "Moved the team to [ASK or given fact], which [given result]."
- If you don't know the outcome: ask, or suggest dropping the line. Never make one up.
- Never hide a fact the reader needs. Rewording isn't lying.

## Self-check (do all four)

1. Every number, tool and product name in a new line appears in the CV or the answers.
2. No verb got bigger than the truth.
3. Agents mentioned at most once in the summary and once per role.
4. Rescore the nine skills with the new lines, and show before and after.
