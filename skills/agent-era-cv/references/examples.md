# Worked examples

Every line here is made up. None is a real person. Words in brackets are empty slots, not facts: [N], [before], [after], [period], [reviewer role], [link]. Never copy a slot or its wording into a real rewrite. In a real rewrite, each slot is filled from the CV or the person's answers, or it becomes `[ASK: ...]`.

## Product manager
- Cut: "Led cross-functional ceremonies", "Owned the roadmap", stakeholder counts.
- Add: prototypes you built yourself, ideas tested per month, eval sets you wrote, time from idea to a real user.
- Old (Example): "Owned the roadmap for the onboarding squad and worked with 4 teams."
- New (Example): "Got [N] onboarding ideas in front of real users in [period], up from [before], by building and testing prototypes myself; proved by [link]."

## Engineer
- Cut: language lists with years next to them.
- Add: what you ship with agents, how you check their work, speed and cost per change.
- Old (Example): "Responsible for backend services using Python and Go."
- New (Example): "Shipped [N] changes a [period], up from [before], with the test pass rate held at [rate], using coding agents checked against a test suite I wrote."

## Designer
- Cut: tool lists, "pixel-perfect".
- Add: directions explored, working prototypes, user tests, time to a tested design.
- Old (Example): "Created high-fidelity mockups in Figma for the checkout redesign."
- New (Example): "Took checkout from brief to a tested design in [after], down from [before], by building working prototypes myself; proved by [N] recorded user tests."

## Data or analyst
- Cut: "Created dashboards", lists of BI tools.
- Add: questions answered per week, time to an answer, how you check AI-written queries.
- Old (Example): "Built weekly KPI dashboards for the sales team."
- New (Example): "Cut weekly reporting from [before] to [after] by building a research agent on [data source]; proved by a [N]-case test set that [reviewer role] signs off."

## Marketer
- Cut: channel lists, team size.
- Add: tests per month, content workflows you built, cost per asset, what checks the output.
- Old (Example): "Managed content calendar across 5 channels."
- New (Example): "Ran [N] content tests a [period], up from [before], by building a draft-and-review workflow and checking every piece against [checklist] before it goes out."

## Ops or founder
- Cut: "Managed processes", "restructured the team to reduce costs".
- Add: workflows built, hours given back and what they went to, errors caught, what you ship alone.
- Old (Example): "Restructured the support team to reduce costs."
- New (Example): "Gave the support team [N] hours a week back for [what the time went to] by building a ticket-routing workflow that I audit [how often]; routing accuracy [after], up from [before]."
- Founder (Example): "Ship updates to [N] live apps [how often] on my own, designing and testing each release and using AI agents to write the code; proved by [link]."

## Bad rewrites, and why

- Bad (Example): "Agents automated weekly reporting using a Claude-powered research pipeline."
- Why it fails: agents are the subject, there's no baseline, nothing to check, and a tool name sits where the result should be.
- Bad (Example): "Cut support costs by replacing [N] support staff with an AI workflow."
- Why it fails: it sells the win as removing people. Lead with what the team could now do, and only with facts the person gave.
- Bad (Example): "Cut reporting from 3 days to 4 hours in Q3."
- Why it fails if the person never said it: every number and the period were invented. Each one must come from the CV or an answer, or be an `[ASK]`.
