# Worked examples

Every line here is a made-up example. None is a real person. Numbers in brackets are slots to fill with real facts. In a real rewrite, any slot you can't fill from the CV or the person's answers becomes `[ASK: ...]`.

## Product manager
- Cut: "Led cross-functional ceremonies", "Owned the roadmap", stakeholder counts.
- Add: prototypes you built yourself, ideas tested per month, eval sets you wrote, time from idea to a real user.
- Old (Example): "Owned the roadmap for the onboarding squad and worked with 4 teams."
- New (Example): "Got [12] onboarding ideas in front of real users in Q3, up from [3] the quarter before, by building and testing prototypes myself; proved by [live demo link]."

## Engineer
- Cut: language lists with years next to them.
- Add: what you ship with agents, how you check their work, speed and cost per change.
- Old (Example): "Responsible for backend services using Python and Go."
- New (Example): "Shipped [20] changes a week, up from [8], with the test pass rate held at [98%], by running coding agents on a test suite I wrote."

## Designer
- Cut: tool lists, "pixel-perfect".
- Add: directions explored, working prototypes, user tests, time to a tested design.
- Old (Example): "Created high-fidelity mockups in Figma for the checkout redesign."
- New (Example): "Took checkout from brief to a tested design in [6] days, down from [3] weeks, by building working prototypes myself; proved by [8] recorded user tests."

## Data or analyst
- Cut: "Created dashboards", lists of BI tools.
- Add: questions answered per week, time to an answer, how you check AI-written queries.
- Old (Example): "Built weekly KPI dashboards for the sales team."
- New (Example): "Cut weekly reporting from [3 days] to [4 hours] by building a research agent on our sales data; proved by a [20]-case test set the finance lead signs off."

## Marketer
- Cut: channel lists, team size.
- Add: campaigns and tests per month, content workflows you built, cost per asset, what checks the output.
- Old (Example): "Managed content calendar across 5 channels."
- New (Example): "Ran [9] content tests a month, up from [2], by building a draft-and-review workflow with [named tool], with every piece checked against our brand checklist before it goes out."

## Ops or founder
- Cut: "Managed processes", "restructured the team to reduce costs".
- Add: workflows built, hours given back and what they went to, errors caught, what you ship alone.
- Old (Example): "Restructured the support team to reduce costs."
- New (Example): "Freed [12] hours a week for customer calls by building a ticket-routing workflow that handles [800+] requests a week at [94%] accuracy; proved by the support lead's weekly audit."
- Founder (Example): "Ship weekly updates to [2] live apps on my own, designing and testing each release and using AI agents to write the code; proved by [public app page]."

## A bad rewrite, and why

- Bad (Example): "Agents automated weekly reporting using a Claude-powered research pipeline."
- Why it fails: agents are the subject, there's no baseline, nothing to check, and a tool name sits where the result should be.
